# Loom Walkthrough Script

Hi, this is my Real Estate Lead Management Dashboard.

I built it to help sales teams track leads, prioritize follow-ups, and understand the pipeline at a glance.

At the top, you can see the core metrics: total leads, closed won leads, conversion rate, and follow-up alerts.

The charts show leads by status and leads by source, so the team can quickly see where pipeline volume is coming from and where deals are getting stuck.

The table below supports search and filtering, so it is easy to find a lead by name, phone, company, source, or status.

I can update a lead status directly from the table, which keeps the pipeline current without extra navigation.

The main product feature here is the Lead Priority Scoring + Follow-Up Alert Engine.

This ranks leads based on source quality, budget, and pipeline stage, and it flags leads that need follow-up within 48 hours.

That helps sales reps focus on the hottest opportunities first and reduces missed follow-ups.

Overall, this is a simple but practical dashboard built for speed, clarity, and business value.
